Aldor Licensing History
=======================

This file records the principal licensing terms used for Aldor distributions.
It is historical information.  The licensing terms for this distribution are
given in LICENSE.

Here "APL" means "Aldor Public License".  It is unrelated to the Apache
License, Version 2.0.

NAG distributions -- 1994-2000
-------------------------------

The compiler was distributed by the Numerical Algorithms Group with Axiom,
first under the names A# and Axiom-XL and later as Aldor.  Those distributions
were governed by the terms accompanying the corresponding NAG products.  They
predate the independent Aldor 1.x licensing described below.

Aldor 1.0 -- 2001
-----------------

Aldor 1.0 was released under the Aldor Public License version 1 (APL1).

Aldor 1.1 -- 2007
-----------------

Aldor 1.1 was released under the Aldor Public License version 2 (APL2).

Aldor 1.2 -- 2013
-----------------

Aldor 1.2 was released as an in-progress development version.  It was made
available under both the Apache License, Version 2.0 and the Aldor Public
License version 2.

aldorlang continuation -- 2013-2026
-----------------------------------

The public `aldorlang/aldor` repository continued from the 2013
Apache-licensed source line.  Its source tree is licensed under the Apache
License, Version 2.0.  Its version was advanced to 1.3.0 in 2021 and to 1.4.0
in 2023.

Aldor 1.5.0 -- 2026
-------------------

Aldor 1.5.0 is released under the Apache License, Version 2.0, except for
components and individual files identified as separately licensed.  The
1.5.0 number acknowledges the intervening public 1.3.0 and 1.4.0 version
lines in `aldorlang/aldor`.

Historical Aldor Public License texts
-------------------------------------

For historical and legal reference, the texts of the old Aldor Public
Licenses are retained in:

    history/LICENSE-APL1
    history/LICENSE-APL2

These historical license files record terms that applied to earlier
distributions.  They do not govern Aldor 1.5.0.
